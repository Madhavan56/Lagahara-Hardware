# Image credits

Product photography for two categories was sourced from Wikimedia Commons
(freely licensed, real photos) rather than the generated placeholder used for
the rest of the catalog. Both licenses require attribution, kept here rather
than on every page.

- **Plywood** — ["Plywood"](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Plywood.jpg),
  Wikimedia Commons, [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/)
- **Mica & Laminates** — ["Formica Laminate for Countertops 01"](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Formica_Laminate_for_Countertops_01.jpg),
  Wikimedia Commons, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

All other product images are generator-created placeholders (`npm run
seed:catalog`), not real photography, and should be replaced with actual
product photos before launch. Re-run `npm run apply:real-images` to reapply
these two if products are reseeded.
